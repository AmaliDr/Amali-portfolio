05 / About

A doctor's eye for why people buy

I've always been fascinated by people. What drives their decisions, what motivates them, and how understanding them can make a real difference.

That curiosity first led me to medicine, where I learned to listen, think critically, and look beyond symptoms to understand the person behind them. But I've always had an entrepreneurial side too. I wanted to build things, solve problems, and create something of my own.

So I did. I built a DTC brand from the ground up, experiencing firsthand the challenges of understanding customers, earning their trust, and turning a good product into a business.

Somewhere along that journey, I discovered my passion for creative strategy.

I realized that the skills I'd developed in medicine and entrepreneurship translated naturally into understanding consumer behavior. The ability to ask better questions, uncover hidden motivations, and connect problems with solutions became the foundation of how I approach advertising.

Today, as a Performance Creative Strategist, I bring those worlds together. I approach creativity with a doctor's curiosity, a founder's commercial mindset, and a genuine desire to help people and businesses grow.

Because to me, great advertising starts with understanding people, not just selling to them.
